# GLOZA // Curated Luxury Sneaker Reselling Vault

A clean, modern, professional footwear e-commerce web platform for **GLOZA**, inspired by modern Dribbble landing pages (such as Heepzy and top contemporary sneaker boutiques). Built using semantic **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript**.

---

## 💎 Design & Visual Philosophy

- **Modern & Professional Light Aesthetic**:
  - Crisp, airy backgrounds (`#f8fafc`, `#ffffff`) with deep slate typography (`#0f172a`, `#475569`).
  - High-end royal blue accents (`#1e40af`, `#2563eb`) that naturally match the Born x Raised SB Dunk and Ja 4 Deep Water colorways.
  - No sci-fi scanlines, heavy neon laser grids, or distracting trailing cursors.
  - Clean drop shadows, soft border radiuses, and refined card micro-interactions.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

1. **Editorial Hero Section**:
   - Asymmetrical modern layout with bold typography (*Syne & Plus Jakarta Sans*).
   - Trust metrics: *100% Authenticity Rate, 12-Pt Lab Inspection, 48-Hr Insured Delivery*.
   - Floating sneaker hero stage featuring the **Nike SB Dunk Low x Born x Raised**, interactive 360° video spin preview bubble, market pricing, and live drop allocation countdown.

2. **Cinematic Drop Studio (Video & 360 Rotation)**:
   - **4K On-Feet & Unboxing Reel**: Embedded video player (`video-unboxing.mp4`) with custom play/pause button, audio unmute, and chapter jump chips (*00:00 Unboxing & Presentation, 00:06 Lacing Up, 00:12 Iridescent Swoosh, 00:18 Court Motion*).
   - **360° Studio Turntable Player**: Interactive 360-degree rotation (`video-spin360.mp4`) with slider scrub, direct mouse/touch drag-to-rotate, live degree readout, preset angle chips (*0° Profile, 90° Heel, 180° Medial, 270° Toe Box*), and Auto-Rotate toggle.

3. **4-Point Sneaker Craftsmanship & Anatomy**:
   - Minimalist interactive beacons over the shoe image:
     - *Point 1*: Shifting Holographic Swoosh & Heel Graphic
     - *Point 2*: Embroidered Fat SB Laces with "In Loving Memory" script
     - *Point 3*: Embossed "ON THE TURF" 3D Midsole
     - *Point 4*: Translucent Ice-Blue Outsole Artwork
   - Clean macro photo viewer with technical specifications table.

4. **The Vault Marketplace Catalog**:
   - Clean category filter pills (*All Grails, Nike SB, Performance / Ja, Air Jordan Vault, Collabs*) with real-time search.
   - Clean product cards with favorite heart toggle, condition pill, size selector chips (`US 8.5` - `US 12`), market value, and "Add to Bag" action.

5. **The Manifesto / Vault Wall Showcase**:
   - Editorial showcase featuring the Jordan 1s vault wall photograph (`gloza-vault-wall.jpg`).
   - The GLOZA Manifesto: *"YOU CAN BUY US OR YOU MAY ACHIEVE ME. WHERE DO YOU FIND TRUE HAPPINESS? FIND THE DIFFERENCE BY GLOZA."*
   - 4 trust pillars: 12-Point Audit, Climate Vault Storage, Smart NFC Tag, and Armored Global Shipping.

6. **Consign & Resell Calculator**:
   - Interactive financial tool for sneaker collectors to calculate instant cash buyout vs. consignment return (86% instant wire vs. 94% consignment sale).

7. **NFC Authenticity Verifier Simulator**:
   - Serial code input (`GLZ-BXR-7721`) with scan animation and verified digital certificate.

8. **Slide-Out Bag Drawer & Checkout**:
   - Clean slide-over cart drawer with quantity removal, promo discount code validator (`GLOZA10` for 10% off), and order confirmation celebration modal.

9. **Quick View & Macro Zoom Modal**:
   - Multi-angle gallery with thumbnail switching and 1.6x cursor-follow zoom lens.
